Common No-Start Causes

We Diagnose the Problem Before Replacing Parts.

A vehicle that will not start can have several different causes. The right first step is finding out which system is actually failing.

Battery

Battery or Connection Problems

Weak batteries, poor terminals, damaged connections and related power-supply problems can prevent the vehicle from starting.

Starter

Starter or Starting Circuit

A failed starter, starter circuit or control problem can cause a click, intermittent crank or complete no-crank condition.

Charging System

Alternator Problems

A charging-system problem can leave the battery discharged and cause the vehicle to shut down or fail to restart.

Electrical / Other

Other No-Start Causes

Fuses, relays, wiring, sensors, immobilizer issues and other faults can also prevent an engine from starting.

No-start symptoms can have more than one possible cause. Diagnosis helps avoid replacing a battery, starter, alternator or other part that is not actually causing the problem.
